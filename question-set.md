

About this research

I am conducting this study as part of my Master’s thesis at HTW Berlin.

The research investigates how AI-mediated discovery — including AI search, conversational assistants and AI-enabled recommendation or discovery interfaces — may affect how consumers discover, compare and evaluate B2C products and services.

The study aims to understand:

* potential competitive risks for firms that do not adapt;
* how practitioners and organizations are currently responding;
* which strategic and organizational capabilities may become important;
* and how these findings can be translated into an actionable framework and implementation roadmap for B2C companies.

This questionnaire is designed as an asynchronous qualitative interview. There are no correct or expected answers. I am interested in your professional experience, observations and judgment.

Where possible, please distinguish between:

what you have directly observed,
what your organization or industry is currently experimenting with, and
what you expect may happen in the future.

Please do not disclose confidential, proprietary or commercially sensitive information about your employer.

Responses will be used solely for academic research purposes and will be handled according to the confidentiality and anonymization arrangements described below.
